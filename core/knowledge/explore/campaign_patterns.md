# General D2C Campaign Patterns

- **Objection-led creative**: Building a script directly around the most common purchase objection (price, "will this work for me", skepticism about claims) rather than only listing benefits.
- **Review-mining**: Turning recurring phrases from customer reviews into ad hooks or headlines, since they're often more persuasive than brand-written copy.
- **Seasonal/occasion tie-ins**: Anchoring a campaign to a recognizable moment (summer, a festival, "back to routine") to borrow existing consumer attention.
- **Retention-focused creative**: Not all creative is for acquisition - post-purchase or repeat-customer messaging (usage tips, routine-building, replenishment reminders) can support retention goals.
- **Category education vs. brand promotion**: Especially in newer categories, some of the highest-performing creative teaches the customer about the category itself before mentioning the brand.
