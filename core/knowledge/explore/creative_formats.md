# Common D2C Creative Formats

- **UGC-style video**: A customer or creator talks to camera about their experience. Feels native to Instagram/TikTok feeds; low production value is often a feature, not a bug.
- **Founder story**: The brand founder explains why the product exists. Builds trust, works well for newer or challenger brands.
- **Before/after or transformation**: Shows a visible change over time (skin, hair, routine). Common in beauty and wellness categories; claims should stay demonstrable and avoid guaranteeing outcomes.
- **Problem/solution skit**: A short, often humorous scene that sets up a relatable problem and resolves it with the product.
- **Comparison**: Positions the product against a generic alternative ("the old way" vs. "the new way") rather than naming competitors directly.
- **Static carousel**: Multi-slide educational or listicle content (e.g. "5 signs you need a new face wash") - works well for top-of-funnel awareness.
- **Influencer/creator collab**: A creator's own format and voice, with light brand direction rather than a fully scripted brief.
