# Sample Brand Deep-Dive: FlexWear - Brand Voice & Positioning

A worked example of a fitness-apparel D2C brand, at the same depth as the SproutMix example - illustrative/fictional, representative of real activewear brands. This brand's compliance/claim rules live in `flexwear_compliance.md` instead of here - they're loaded deterministically into every request for this brand, not retrieved like this file.

## Brand foundation & voice

FlexWear is a direct-to-consumer activewear brand. Brand purpose: make movement feel accessible, not intimidating - the promise is confidence in your own body, not a performance or aesthetic outcome. Tagline: "Move like you mean it."

Archetype: Everyperson layered with Hero ("you have what it takes, right now" rather than "become someone else"). Tone - do: direct, body-neutral, real bodies in real motion, technical-but-plain-language fabric explanations. Tone - avoid: fitspiration guilt, before/after body-transformation framing, diet-culture language, "no excuses" intensity-shaming.

Pricing & positioning: mid-premium, cost-per-wear framing over discount-led messaging ("this pair replaces three worse ones," not "50% off today only"). Avoid urgency language on evergreen product; urgency framing is acceptable only on genuinely time-boxed drops (new colorway launch), never implied on always-in-stock core SKUs.

## Product line at a glance

Three lines, differentiated by use case rather than age band:

| | Core | Power | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use case | Everyday wear, light activity | High-intensity training, compression | Post-workout, loungewear |
| Fabric | 4-way stretch, moisture-wicking | High-compression, sweat-wicking, squat-proof opacity | Soft-touch, breathable |
| Price tier | Entry | Premium | Mid |
| Approved distinct claim | "All-day comfort, gym to errands" | "Tested for squat-proof opacity" (only where actual fabric-opacity testing has been done) | "Designed for recovery, not just rest" |

Sizing runs XS-4XL across all three lines - inclusive sizing is a core differentiator, not an add-on line.
