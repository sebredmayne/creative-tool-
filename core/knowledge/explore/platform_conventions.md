# Platform Conventions (General)

- **Instagram Reels / TikTok**: Hook in the first 1-2 seconds, vertical 9:16, captions on-screen (many viewers watch muted), short overall length (15-30s) tends to perform well for awareness.
- **Instagram feed/carousel**: Slower pace than Reels, good for educational or listicle content; the first slide needs a strong headline since it's the only one shown in-feed.
- **YouTube pre-roll/mid-roll**: Longer attention span than short-form, can support a slower narrative build.
- **Influencer whitelisting/spark ads**: Running a creator's organic post as a paid ad from the brand's own ad account - keeps the creator's authentic voice while adding paid reach.
- General note: platform conventions shift often - treat these as starting heuristics, not fixed rules, and validate against current in-platform performance data where available.
